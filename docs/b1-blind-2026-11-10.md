# B1 blind test results (16a section 5.3), 2026-11-10

Question: can a strong model tell generated test invoices from real inbox items?
Pass: accuracy at 0.5 at most 0.60 and ROC AUC at most 0.65.

## Result

| Run | Judge | Corpus | Accuracy | Wilson 95% | AUC | Pass |
|:-|:-|:-|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Primary | `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` (spec default) | 50 real + 50 test | 0.59 | 0.492 to 0.681 | 0.636 | yes, narrowly |
| Second judge | `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017` | stratified 25 real + 25 test | 0.50 | 0.366 to 0.634 | 0.530 | yes, but uninformative |

The primary run passes by 0.01 on accuracy and 0.014 on AUC. The Wilson upper bound of 0.68 is above the 0.60 line, so this is not a robust pass. Section 5.3 already asks for an n = 200 rerun before quoting a number in the README.

The second judge is degenerate. It gave every item a p_test between about 0.95 and 0.99, real or test. Its accuracy of 0.50 is the base rate, not evidence of indistinguishability.

## Corpus

| Set | Count | Source |
|:-|:-:|:-|
| R1, R2, R3 | 25, 15, 10 | `scripts/fixtures/b1-real.json`. `bun b1-validate.ts` reports ok with 50 items, 15 vendors, 0 errors and 5 fresh R3 payees |
| T | 50 | `scripts/fixtures/b1-tests.json`, from `scripts/b1-generate.ts` |

Generation used the production tester path. It took the vendor's public style set, drew the amount with `sampleAmount` from style-set totals, created a fresh payee with `newTestPayee` (30 NEAR implicit, 20 eth-implicit), and ran the real-intake normaliser with the vendor_id rule. Vendors follow the real set's vendor distribution. Categories rotate evenly, 10 each. The amount cap was `B1_MAX_PER_PAYMENT=100000` USDC, so it never bound.

Generator model: `Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B-abliterated`, the first entry of `DEFAULT_GENERATOR_MODELS`. It produced all 50 items. Fallbacks were configured but never used, and no draft was rejected.

The harness's strict policy accepts only NEAR-hosted vLLM models with model attestation. `z-ai/glm-5.3` (tinfoil), `openai/*` and `anthropic/*` fail before any request with `policy.model_attestation_required`. So no judge clearly stronger than GLM 5.3 Flash can be used without loosening verification, and the policy was left unchanged. DeepSeek V4.1 Flash is the only verifiable non-Qwen alternative.

## Label leakage found, for the next run to fix

Share of items with each feature:

| Feature | Real | Test | Fix |
|:-|:-:|:-:|:-|
| `chain.exists` true | 0.90 | 0.00 | By design (16a 3.3). It is a genuine fraud signal, but it marks every test. Consider pre-creating some lure payees or mixing in existing ones |
| Named payee, not implicit | 0.56 | 0.00 | Same root cause. Tests always pay a 64-hex or 0x account |
| Email body repeats the payee account | 0.20 | 0.98 | The generator prompt says the invoice must ask for payment to the payee. Let the email omit it or refer to the invoice |
| ISO date in email body | 0.00 | 0.72 | Real emails write dates like "28 Aug 2026". Add this to the style instructions, or give email samples |
| " - " separator in subject | 0.00 | 0.50 | Same: the generator gets invoice samples but no email samples |
| Issued dates | Aug to Nov 2026 | Jun to Aug 2026 | Generated dates copy the style samples. Pass today's date to the generator |
| Filler line to hit the sampled amount | rare | common | For example "Payment processing fee 4.11" or "Bank account change credit -8.00". Let the model choose the line amounts and set the total after, or sample whole line quantities |
| Arithmetic consistent | 50 of 50 | 48 of 50 | Reject drafts whose lines plus tax do not equal the total, as `b1-validate.ts` does for real items |
| from_email equals the vendor contact | 0.84 | 0.98 | `lookalike_vendor` drafts mostly use the exact vendor address |

The judge prompt also gives the judge `chain.exists`. Only 4 test reasons and 19 real reasons cited it, so the primary judge did not exploit it much. A judge told about the 5.3 payee rule would.

## Harness changes

`scripts/b1-blind.ts` now writes receipts after every call and prints progress to stderr. It also runs `B1_CONCURRENCY` calls at once (default 4) and retries a call after `B1_CALL_TIMEOUT_S` (default 300), up to `B1_ATTEMPTS` tries (default 3). Each score is saved by item id next to the receipts, and `B1_RESUME=1` keeps saved scores and receipts. Scoring and the prompt are unchanged. The first full run was lost because the old harness wrote nothing until all 100 items were judged and the process hit a time limit. That run is the main reason total model calls came to roughly 250 instead of about 150: 51 generation, roughly 45 in the lost run, 6 probes and 151 in the two final runs, one of them a timeout retry.

## Reproduce

```bash
cd scripts
bun b1-validate.ts
bun --env-file=../backend/.env.tester b1-generate.ts
JUDGE_MODEL=z-ai/glm-5.3-flash B1_CORPUS=fixtures/b1-real.json,fixtures/b1-tests.json B1_RECEIPTS_OUT=out/b1-receipts.json bun --env-file=../backend/.env.tester b1-blind.ts
JUDGE_MODEL=deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 B1_CORPUS=out/b1-subset-real.json,out/b1-subset-tests.json B1_RECEIPTS_OUT=out/b1-receipts-deepseek.json bun --env-file=../backend/.env.tester b1-blind.ts
```

The subset files hold the item ids used here. They were drawn with Python `random.seed(7)`: 13 R1, 7 R2 and 5 R3 items, plus 5 tests per category. Regenerating tests produces new payees and new text, so a rerun measures a fresh sample, not these exact items. The primary run takes about 35 minutes with concurrency 4, at about 50 seconds per call.
